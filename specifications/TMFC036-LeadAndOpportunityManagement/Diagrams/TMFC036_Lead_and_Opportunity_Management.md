# TMFC036 – Lead And Opportunity Management

## 1. Overview

| Component Name | ID | Description | ODA Function Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead And Opportunity Management | TMFC036 | Lead and Opportunity Management component provides the functionality for lead and opportunity capture, qualification, reporting, and support during the pre-sales stage. | PartyManagement |
