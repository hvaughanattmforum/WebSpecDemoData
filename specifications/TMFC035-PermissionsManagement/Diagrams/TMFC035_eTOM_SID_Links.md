# TMFC035 eTOM–SID Links

Source: transcribed from the original PDF's "2.3. eTOM L2 - SID ABEs links" diagram (page 5 of 13).

This component has no eTOM business activities at all — `componentMetadata.eTOMs` is empty (`[]`) in the
current YAML, and the PDF's own section 2.1 has no eTOM activity table either. The diagram itself matches
this: it contains no dashed "eTOM Business Activity" box, only SID Data Entity cylinders, some belonging to
this component (grouped in the "TMFC035 - Party Roles & Permissions Management" box) and some to other
components (grouped in "TMFC028 - Party Management" and "TMFC020 - Digital Identity Management" boxes).

There are therefore no eTOM-to-SID cross-framework links to record here. Every link on the diagram is
SID-ABE-to-SID-ABE, and those are out of scope for this file.
