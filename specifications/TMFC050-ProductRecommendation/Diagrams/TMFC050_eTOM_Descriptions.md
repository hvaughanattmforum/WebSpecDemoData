# TMFC050 eTOM Business Activity Descriptions

Source: transcribed from `TMFC050 Product Recommendation Management v1.2.0.docx`, section 2.1 (eTOM business activities).

| Identifier | Description | Version | Document Name | Alignment Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.12.9.4 | Cross/Up Sell ensures that the value of the relationship between the customer or other party and an enterprise is maximized by selling additional, or more of the existing, product offerings. The ongoing analysis of customer or other party trends, such as usage, problems, complaints, is used to identify when the current offerings may no longer be appropriate or when the opportunity for a larger sale arises. Based on the data collected, more appropriate or other offering(s) may be recommended to the customer or other party. | v26.0 | Cross/Up Sell | Not declared in component YAML — version defaulted to v26.0 |
| 1.12.9.4.2 | Recommend Appropriate Product Offering(s) recommends more appropriate or other product offering(s) to the customer or other party that may be based on the results of Analyze Customer or Other Party Trends or based on known other offerings that represent an upsell. / For example, a potential new partner may be offered the opportunity to have there logo and contact information added to an enterprise's application that manages a IoT device. | v26.0 | Recommend Appropriate Product Offering(s) | Not declared in component YAML — version defaulted to v26.0 |
