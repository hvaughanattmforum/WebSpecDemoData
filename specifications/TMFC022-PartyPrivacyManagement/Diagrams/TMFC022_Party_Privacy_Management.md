# TMFC022 – Party Privacy Management

## 1. Overview

| Component Name | ID | Description | ODA Function Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Party Privacy Management | TMFC022 | The Party Privacy Management component defines the privacy policy rules established by the CSP (according to applicable regulations such as GDPR in Europe); applies these rules to each Party interacting with the CSP and to all of their personal information and personally identifiable information; and registers explicit opt-in and opt-out given by Parties regarding the use of their personal information for dedicated purposes, such as marketing. | PartyManagement |
