# TMFC043 – Fault Management

## 1. Overview

| Component Name | ID | Description | ODA Function Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fault Management | TMFC043 | Fault Management component involves identifying, diagnosing, and resolving issues that prevent the communication resources and their overlaying services from functioning correctly. Its scope covers this functionality at the ODA Production layer while business related resolutions are not part of its scope. The Fault Management component manages the currently active alarms or problem candidates, potentially modifying or enhancing their data to make persistent alarms more actionable. It may apply logic to preserve alarms that reflect actual problems, eliminating those that are merely symptoms. Various actions can be taken using the alarm/problem information as part of the resolution process, such as applying automated/automonous corrective actions or opening trouble tickets. | Production |
