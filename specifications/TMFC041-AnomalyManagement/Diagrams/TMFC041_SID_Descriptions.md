| Anomaly | Anomaly ABE represents any unusual or unexpected event, observation, pattern, or item that is significantly different from the norm that an enterprise wishes to detect, analyze, assess and mitigate within an autonomous environment. In order for the autonomous operations environment to behave within the calibrated operational level, anomalies may require administrative and operational guidance.
