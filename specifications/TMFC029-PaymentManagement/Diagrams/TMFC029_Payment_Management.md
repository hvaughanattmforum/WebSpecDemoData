# TMFC029 – Payment Management

## 1. Overview

| Component Name | ID | Description | ODA Function Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment Management | TMFC029 | Payment Management component manages methods used by parties to make payments. It also administers payment plans, handles payments, assigns payments against bills, settlements, and other objects requiring payments. | PartyManagement |
