| 198 | Customer Loyalty Score Balance Management function calculates the score according to accumulation/decrease rules. When a customer subscribes to the loyalty program with more than one SIM or other ‘traffic objects’, the Score Management accumulates the points into a single balance. The loyalty score could decrease for one of the following events: prize purchase, points expiry or points deletion by Call Centre. The functionality may also include the visualization of Score details (date, description event type, points, final score) via different contact Channels (e.g. via Web, IVR, Call Centre). | ProductRepository Management | Loyalty Account Management | v24.0 | Customer Loyalty Score Balance Management | Matches YAML (v24.0) |
